# Product

## Register

product

## Users
- **Primary Users**: Civitas akademika Universitas Sam Ratulangi (UNSRAT), including students, lecturers, and academic staff seeking precise information about academic regulations, calendars, and institutional profile.
- **Secondary Users**: Researchers and evaluators (such as the developer) who use the Ragas evaluation tab to analyze retrieval parameters, Wilcoxon test results, and audit logs.

## Product Purpose
A RAG (Retrieval-Augmented Generation) chatbot system designed to answer academic queries with high accuracy, backed by official university documents (such as Peraturan Rektor No. 01 Tahun 2025). The application provides a dual-tab workspace: a conversational AI chatbot interface with inline citations and a data-driven evaluation dashboard.

## Brand Personality
- **Voice & Tone**: Professional, helpful, academically precise, and authoritative.
- **Visual Identity**: Warm, dignified, and traditional yet clean. Anchored by the official Maroon Klasik UNSRAT theme (primary `#7B2D2D` with warm white `#FAFAF8` surfaces).
- **Keywords**: Authoritative, Trustworthy, Clean.

## Anti-references
- **Neon/Futuristic Chatbots**: Avoid glowing blue/cyan borders, glowing text, and generic "AI sci-fi" aesthetics.
- **Tech-SaaS Clichés**: Avoid generic cream-beige backgrounds (without specific brand tinting), rounded elements for the sake of it, or side-stripe colored borders on cards.
- **AI Scaffolding Tells**: Avoid tiny uppercase tracked eyebrows on every section or numbered section markers (01, 02) where no ordered sequence exists.
- **Information Buffet**: Do not clutter the screen with unnecessary decoration; focus on readability and clean spacing.

## Design Principles
1. **Academic Authority**: The visual styling must command trust. Typography should use distinct weight contrasts, and spacing must feel intentional and structured.
2. **Accountability & Integrity**: Answers must map directly to source citations. The UI must present inline citations clearly and associate them transparently with reference snippets.
3. **Seamless Dualism**: The split between the interactive chatbot and the analytic evaluation panels must feel cohesive, sharing the same Maroon Klasik design system.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
- **High Contrast**: Ensure body text and placeholder text maintain a contrast ratio of at least 4.5:1 against their backgrounds.
- **Refinement in Motion**: All transitions and animations must support `@media (prefers-reduced-motion: reduce)` by transitioning instantly or with simple crossfades.
- **Layout Adaptability**: Typographic elements must use `text-wrap: balance` on headings and `pretty` on paragraphs to eliminate orphans and prevent text overflows on narrower viewports.
